<task type="auto" id="03-01-T3">
  <name>Build Judging Service, HMAC Callback Processor & Bounded Polling Fallback</name>
  <description>
    Implement `backend/app/services/judging/service.py`:
    - `dispatch_submission`: Dispatches testcases to `JudgeProvider` in batch, creates `SubmissionRun` records with `judge0_token`, sets submission to RUNNING.
    - `handle_judge_callback`: Validates HMAC signature, finds corresponding `SubmissionRun`, records verdict/time/memory, and evaluates if all test cases have finished.
    - `aggregate_verdicts`: Evaluates verdict priority matrix (SYSTEM_ERROR > COMPILATION_ERROR > First failing test: TLE > MLE > OLE > RE > WA > ACCEPTED) and updates submission.
    - `poll_pending_submission`: Bounded polling querying Judge0 for incomplete test runs to recover dropped callbacks.
  </description>
  <verify>
    `python -c "from app.services.judging.service import JudgingService; print('JudgingService loaded')"`
  </verify>
  <done>Judging lifecycle, callback processing, verdict aggregation, and polling fallback implemented.</done>
